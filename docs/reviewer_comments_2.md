Your manuscript, "Model-based evaluation of an infant HPV prophylactic vaccination program in Nigeria", has now been assessed.

Thank you for addressing the reviewer comments, they have a few more suggestion below. Furthermore, several revisions would strengthen the methodological transparency and ensure the conclusions remain appropriately supported by the evidence:


1) Please provide additional sensitivity analyses around the assumed education-related vaccination and screening uptake differences, as these assumptions are key drivers of the equity-related conclusions.
2) Please further emphasise that infant vaccine efficacy at exposure (50%, 70%, and 95%) represents hypothetical scenario assumptions rather than evidence-based estimates, and ensure this is clearly communicated throughout the Abstract, Results, and Discussion.
3) Please expand the description of model calibration and goodness-of-fit, including how the best-fitting parameter sets were selected and how calibration uncertainty was incorporated.
4) Please consider reporting broader uncertainty intervals (for example, 95% uncertainty intervals) alongside interquartile ranges to better reflect uncertainty in long-term projections.
5) Please provide additional justification for the assumptions underpinning screening scale-up to 70% coverage and discuss the practical feasibility of achieving this level of uptake in Nigeria.
6) Please clarify the extent to which other important determinants of vaccine and screening access (such as wealth, geography, and healthcare access) may influence outcomes beyond the education-related effects incorporated in the model.
7) Please further moderate policy-oriented conclusions regarding infant HPV vaccination and emphasise that the findings are exploratory, hypothesis-generating, and dependent on currently unverified assumptions regarding infant immunogenicity, durability, and programme implementation.
8) Please ensure that all key scenario parameters and model inputs are sufficiently documented to maximise reproducibility alongside the publicly available code repository.
